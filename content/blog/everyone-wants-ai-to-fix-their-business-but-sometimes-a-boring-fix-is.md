A small business owner spent 3 hours daily running through WhatsApp and Google Forms just to keep the business going. I mapped out the whole flow and found out 2 of those hours could run by itself.

It serves a community, and this process repeats daily📋:

9am: Blast a Google Form to multiple groups to collect orders. Meanwhile, customers submit a screenshot of their TNG payment.

12pm: Close orders, download the Google Form, manually compile the order list and check the payment screenshots. Group them by location, then turn them into two long texts: one for the vendor to prepare and pack the food, one for the delivery guys on which food to take and where to deliver.

Then the delivery guys send photo proof into a WhatsApp group when he/she finished the delivery.

Looks legit and it works, but it’s a super-duper manual process.

Here’s what could be automated instead:

- Vendor sends in what’s available that day, AI picks it up, understands it, and compiles it into the Google Form. Owner previews, approves, and publishes. [1 hr → 15 mins]

- Once the form closes, it auto downloads and generates the vendor and driver lists through a planned data flow. Owner approves, sent. [1 hr → 15 mins]

- For payment checks, the system compares expected vs actual amounts and flags only the differences, with AI helping to speed it up. Owner decides if it’s worth chasing. [1 hr → 30 mins]

- A simple web app for the delivery guys to snap and upload photo proof, sorted by day.

With that said, this isn’t just about food orders 👉 Any business running the same daily loop (collect, compile, check, dispatch, confirm) can be automated the same way. A tuition centre, a laundry service, a cleaning company, a small logistics team. In the era of AI, this kind of copy-paste work honestly shouldn’t still be done by hand.
